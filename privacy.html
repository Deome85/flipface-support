Your people stay on your device

FlipFace helps you remember people by connecting names, photos and personal context. Your collection is stored on your device. FlipFace does not provide a hosted collection, a FlipFace account, or automatic cloud synchronization.

The information you choose to save can include names, photos, organizations, categories, memory cues and notes about people. FlipFace uses this information to display your collection, organize and filter people, and run practice games. It is not uploaded to a FlipFace server, sold, or used for advertising.

Contacts and photos

When you choose to import contacts, FlipFace uses Apple's contact picker and receives the contacts you select. The current iOS import copies available names, nicknames, organizations and thumbnail photos for review. It does not maintain a live connection to your address book or synchronize later contact changes.

You can choose a photo or take one with the camera. FlipFace processes imported photos on the device and re-encodes the stored image without the original photo's EXIF/GPS metadata. The original photo in your library is not changed by this step.

Only save information about others that you have a legitimate reason to keep. Please do not send their private details to support when asking for help.

Storage and app protection

Saved person content and imported photos are encrypted on the device. The local collection key is stored using the device's secure storage. Preferences such as theme and reminder settings are stored locally; not every preference is encrypted.

Optional App lock asks your device to authenticate you using supported biometrics or the device passcode. FlipFace receives an authentication result, not your biometric template or passcode. No storage or security measure eliminates all risk.

Backups and recovery

Backups are manual. You can export a password-protected backup file to a location you choose. If you choose iCloud Drive or another cloud file provider, that provider handles the file under its own terms and privacy practices. FlipFace does not operate that storage or receive the backup password.

Keep both your backup and its password safe. FlipFace cannot reset a forgotten backup password or recover a collection from its own servers. Uninstalling the app, clearing app data, losing the device, or losing its keys can make local data unrecoverable. Restoring App Store purchases does not restore your people or photos.

Purchases

Where purchases are available, Apple handles App Store payments and purchase authentication. FlipFace uses Apple's verified purchase information on the device to determine whether Plus is available. We do not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password. Store ownership information is not used to upload or identify your private collection. Apple's processing of transactions is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications and tracking

Practice reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in FlipFace or in iOS notification settings.

FlipFace does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or an app-operated analytics service. Apple may provide app-distribution or diagnostic information through its developer services under Apple's policies and your device settings. This is separate from sending your people collection to us.

Deleting information

You can delete people from within FlipFace. Deleted records stop appearing in the collection. Deleting a person does not delete the original phone contact or photo, and it does not remove copies from backups you previously exported. Delete those files separately from the locations where you saved them if you no longer need them. We do not promise forensic erasure of old encrypted bytes from device storage.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact flipfaceapp@gmail.com, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address and message. We use it to answer your request and troubleshoot problems. Do not send passwords, unredacted receipts, private contact collections, or backup files. Screenshots should hide other people's details.

Support email is handled through Gmail, provided by Google. Joshua Elliff uses these messages only for support and privacy requests. Resolved conversations are normally deleted within 12 months, except when needed for an ongoing issue or legal obligation. You can use the same contact address for privacy questions about information you have sent directly to us. We cannot read or remotely delete your on-device collection.

This website and policy changes

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. These pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, account registration or file-upload forms. Visiting this website does not send your FlipFace collection to us. GitHub and Google process website requests and support email under their own privacy policies.

We may update this policy when the app or our practices change. The published policy will show its effective date and remain available from the app's listing.
