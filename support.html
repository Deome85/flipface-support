Let’s make it easier.

Quick answers for adding people, practicing names and protecting your collection.

Can I import contacts instead of adding everyone manually?

Yes. Choose the contacts-import option in the add-person flow. Select people, review their details, and save them to your collection. You can also search for contacts and add them to the import batch. Add a memory cue or something you learned about each person to make your next practice session more useful.

Imports are copies, not ongoing synchronization with your phone's contacts.

How do I practice just one group of people?

Use the filters to narrow your collection by category or organization. Multiple filters can be selected. On Study, narrow the practice collection before starting a game. Pin frequently used categories so they're easier to reach.

What are the study games for?

Flip cards and matching games help connect faces with names and personal context. Try a short session before a meeting, gathering or event. They are practice tools, not a test of your worth or a medical treatment.

How do I protect or back up my people?

Open Help and settings → App lock to enable device authentication. For a manual backup, open Help and settings → Backup & restore and save a password-protected file somewhere you can find again. Check that the file saved; if you chose cloud storage, allow its upload to finish. Keep the password safe.

Backups do not update automatically. Export a new one after important changes. Restoring a backup replaces the current collection after confirmation; export your current collection first if you need to keep it.

Can you recover my lost collection or backup password?

FlipFace has no server copy of your people and cannot reset your backup password. Your recovery route is your own encrypted backup and its password. Do not uninstall the app as a troubleshooting step before making a backup.

How do I stop reminders?

Open Help and settings → Practice reminders, or change FlipFace's notification permission in your iPhone settings. Reminders are optional and scheduled locally.

What happens when I delete a person?

The person is removed from FlipFace's collection. The original contact or photo in other apps is not deleted. Existing exported backups are independent files; delete them separately if necessary.
